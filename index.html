😊About Me

  • I am a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). I am also a research intern at Alibaba DAMO Academy, working on multimodal large language models for medical imaging.
  • My research interests include computer vision, multimodal large language models, medical imaging, and zero-shot anomaly detection.
  • I received my M.S. degree in Control Science and Engineering from UESTC and my B.S. degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Chengdu University of Technology.
